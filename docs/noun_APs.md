**b0** has **ъ** as vowel stem. It is visible in **n(a) sg** and **g pl**.
Its dli ov-forms now behavior analogically to **c?**
which we call a property of '0', not of 'b'.
It is 'b' and not 'c' because of non-enclinomen-ness (I hope).
Also not 'c' because all historical 'c's have long -a- like in _па̑њ_.
Has only 2 nouns, one with exceptions. Previously also had _šav_, _lav_.
